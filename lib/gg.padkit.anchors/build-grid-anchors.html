buildGridAnchors

fun <T> buildGridAnchors(rows: List<List<T>>, buttonSize: Float, origin: Offset = Offset.Zero, columnStep: Offset = Offset(1f, 0f), rowStep: Offset = Offset(0.25f, 1f), rotationDegrees: Float = 0.0f): PersistentList<Anchor<T>>

Build a grid, including staggered or slanted rows, in control-local coordinates.

origin locates the first item. columnStep and rowStep are vectors between adjacent items; they need not be perpendicular. Rotation is clockwise in screen coordinates, around the control origin. Ragged/empty rows are allowed and preserve their row index. IDs and layout dimensions have no platform or console-specific meaning.
